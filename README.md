# Devil Cam

A Devil Media Group app for watching home CCTV on phones, tablets, Fire TV and Android TV boxes.

Version 1 works with Hikvision recorders and cameras over the home Wi-Fi:

- **Find my recorder** on the Wi-Fi (Hikvision SADP, with a network check as backup)
- One-time login, stored encrypted on the device
- **Live grid** of every camera (light sub-stream), tap or press OK for full screen at full quality
- **Snapshot** to Pictures/Devil Cam
- **Playback** of recordings by camera, day and time
- **Safety check**: weak password, UPnP, software updates. No port forwarding needed.

Every push to `main` builds a signed APK and publishes it under **Releases**.

Planned: viewing away from home, motion alerts, more camera brands, Alexa / Echo Show skill, Devil TV integration.
